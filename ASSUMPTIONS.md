# Assumptions and decisions

This file records every point where the article (IHRAS, Elsevier cas-dc
manuscript) is silent or ambiguous, the decision taken in this code, the
justification, and the expected impact on the results. "2025" refers to the
precursor by the same group: Rodrigues et al., *Bioengineering* 12(8):795
(2025), doi:10.3390/bioengineering12080795. That article describes the same
sample and the same models in more detail.

| ID | Gap in the article | Decision | Justification | Expected impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The strata are described only as "age (25-year intervals), radiological findings, sex, view position". | Strata = age bin × full `Finding Labels` string × sex × view; cap of 370 per stratum; all images of smaller strata (2025 Sec. 3.1). | This definition gives 50,172 images with `Data_Entry_2017_v2020.csv`, close to the reported 50,133. | The residual difference (39 images; largest stratum 9,605 vs. 9,677) is consistent with the 2017 metadata, whose erroneous ages (> 400 years, mentioned in 2025) were corrected in v2020. With the original `Data_Entry_2017.csv` the count should be closer. |
| A2 | Seed of the sampling not stated. | `SEED = 42`. | Determinism. | Composition changes with the seed; the order of magnitude of the representativeness tests does not. |
| A3 | "SAR-Net trained on ChestX-Det, 14 anatomical regions". SAR-Net (Lian et al. 2021) detects diseases. | TorchXRayVision PSPNet trained on ChestX-Det (`xrv.baseline_models.chestx_det.PSPNet`). | Its 14 targets are exactly the 14 structures listed in 2025 Sec. 3.3. | None on the pipeline; the attribution in the article should be corrected. |
| A4 | Whether the CNNs were trained. | Used pretrained, without training ("default configurations", 2025 Sec. 3.2). | Stated in 2025. | — |
| A5 | Decision threshold and averaging of precision, recall, F1, and specificity. | Threshold 0.55 (2025 Sec. 4.1.1); micro averaging (pooled TP/FP/FN/TN); macro and the F1-optimal threshold also reported. | 2025 reports F1 0.34 at 0.55, which is consistent with micro averaging. | Large: micro-F1 near 0.34 (2025), not 0.911. |
| A6 | Unit of the paired *t*-test, Wilcoxon, McNemar, and ANOVA; ANOVA df (2, 147) imply 50 units per model that are not described. | *t* and Wilcoxon on per-image sample-F1 (n = sample size); McNemar on per-image exact-match correctness; ANOVA on the 5 fold-level metrics (df = 2, 12). | Defensible paired units; the article's df cannot be reconstructed. | *t* and d will differ from the reported values. |
| A7 | Cross-validation of pretrained models. | Patient-grouped, label-stratified 5 folds; the threshold is selected on 4 folds and applied to the held-out fold. | The only meaningful CV without retraining. | — |
| A8 | Definitions of "localization accuracy", "false-positive rate", and "anatomical coverage", and their reference. 2025 Sec. 1.1 states that quantitative validation of the maps is impossible without pixel annotations. | Reference: the 984 NIH bounding boxes. Localization accuracy is the pointing game; FPR is the share of the binarized map outside the box; coverage is the share inside the anatomical masks. Binarization threshold 0.5 of the normalized map. | Standard definitions in the saliency-evaluation literature. | Values will differ from Table 2. |
| A9 | Robustness metric. | Relative drop of micro-F1 (%) on a random subset (default 2,000 images); SNR on the [0, 1] image; photometric change scales contrast and brightness by (1 + δ). | Simplest reading of Sec. 4.4. | — |
| A10 | Fairness tests and age bins (Fig. 12 uses 18–40/41–65/65+; the sampling uses 25-year bins; NIH includes patients under 18). | Both bin sets reported; chi-square of exact-match correctness across subgroups and maximum pairwise Cohen's h (as in 2025). | Reproduces the 2025 analysis and the 2026 figure. | — |
| A11 | SNOMED CT mapping table not published (only 3 codes in Sec. 3.1). | Since run03, `resources/snomed_map.csv` maps the 14 findings and the 14 segmented structures; each code was looked up on tx.fhir.org (International Edition 2025-02-01): active concept, fully specified name in the `display` column. Choices: NIH "Hernia" → Hiatal hernia; "Weasand" → trachea; clavicle, scapula and hilum without laterality. Code 90572001 of Sec. 3.1 is "Structure of lower lobe of lung" (no laterality); "lower lobe of right lung" is 266005. The prompt carries `<finding> : 363698007 \|Finding site\| = <region>` expressions, with the region chosen as in Sec. 3.1 (structure with the largest share of Grad-CAM saliency of the most probable finding). | Inventing clinical codes is not acceptable; every code is checked against the official release. | Mapping accuracy (96.3%) cannot be computed without the authors' gold standard. The published CPU run and run01/run02 used the 3-code map with no region. |
| A12 | LLM variant, access mode, and decoding parameters; full prompt text. | DeepSeek-R1 via the DeepSeek API (`deepseek-reasoner`), or local `DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B` with greedy decoding; CRISPE components from 2025 Table 2. | The full R1 (671B parameters) cannot run on a single V100. | LLM outputs are not deterministic through the API; timing depends on the backend. |
| A13 | Rules of the "rule-based synthetic references" for BLEU. | `report.rule_based_reference`: one sentence listing the predicted findings and the region. | Documented and transparent. | Lexical scores depend on this choice. |
| A14 | Human ratings (5-reader panel, 150-report review, blinded assessment of 100 reports, review time). | Analysis code with input templates in `resources/templates/`; NOT COMPUTED until the authors provide the data. | No public data exist. | κ = 0.847, ICC = 0.923, the 23% reduction, and Table 6 cannot be verified. |
| A15 | Evaluation on images used to train the weights. | The sample includes images of the NIH training split of `densenet121-res224-nih`. The official patient-wise test split (`test_list.txt`) is available in `data.official_split` for a leakage-free evaluation. | Leakage control. | The leakage-free evaluation is expected to be lower. |
| A16 | Bootstrap replicates for the confidence intervals. | B = 1,000, resampling patients. | Common default; patient resampling respects within-patient correlation. | — |
